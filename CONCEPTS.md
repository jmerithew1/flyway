## Panel & research digests (from 2026-08-24 scoping)

- Jam winners: one mechanic fully explored, explainable in a sentence, demoable in 10s; polish is the universal winner trait; missing audio sinks every category.
- Peak-end rule: design the demo around one engineered peak + a deliberate closing image.
- Technical category opportunity: many ambitious entries ship broken — a small **finished** technical gimmick beats a big broken one.
- Stack: Vite + TS browser game; deploy to Vercel/Netlify. Cost ≈ $0 (+~$10 Suno optional).
- Execution contract (learned the hard way): concepts are judged on words + ONE high-fidelity key-art mock; the chosen game gets hours of shader-grade execution before any further judgment.
